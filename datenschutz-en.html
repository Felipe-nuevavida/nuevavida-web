4. Cookies, local storage and consent management

The DE, EN and FR shop pages currently do not embed services for analytics, marketing, external media or personalised advertising. Therefore, we do not use cookies or similar technologies for those purposes.

The first language page you open displays a locally delivered cookie and privacy notice. When you select “Understood”, we store only the acknowledgement status, time and notice version in your browser's local storage. This technically necessary storage is used only to avoid showing the notice repeatedly when you switch between DE, EN and FR or return to one of those pages. You can remove this local storage at any time through your browser settings.

If optional services are added in the future, a consent banner will appear before they are loaded, with equally prominent options to accept or continue without consent. The choice, time and settings version will then be stored locally in your browser. Consent can then be changed or withdrawn at any time through “Privacy settings” on all language pages.

Our “Buy on Amazon” buttons are simple links to Amazon.de. A connection to Amazon is established only after you actively click one of those buttons. Amazon then receives at least your IP address and the information that the link was accessed from this site. Amazon is responsible for subsequent processing; see Amazon's privacy information.

6. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the data you provide, including your email address and request content, in order to handle and respond to your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns a contract and otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data after the request has been completed unless statutory retention obligations apply.
